---
name: Involv service taxonomy
description: Service-type and programme labels used to classify opportunities.
---

Each opportunity has one service type: Direct, Indirect, Advocacy, or Research.
Programme tags are IB DP and HSP; HSP means High School Program.

**Why:** The user chose one service type per listing and clarified the HSP
abbreviation while defining the catalogue filters.

**How to apply:** Keep service type single-select. Programme tags may be
multi-select, but only assign them when the school has confirmed the activity
counts toward that programme. Keep listing image uploads disabled until the
school approves their use and public visibility.
