---
name: Involv content principles
description: Product language decisions for opportunity value and student-facing messaging.
---

Involv should present every meaningful service-learning opportunity as valuable; do not classify listings with a separate CV-quality or CV-relevance label.

**Why:** The user considers all meaningful service-learning experience relevant, and the label can imply that some opportunities matter less.

**How to apply:** Prefer concrete skills, impact, responsibilities, and experience details when explaining an opportunity's value.

Organisers are expected to join Involv and post their own activities. A supplied
school opportunity list can inform preparation, but is not permission to publish
listings or assign ownership on their behalf.

**Why:** The user clarified that real activity content will arrive through the
organisers themselves, rather than being pre-populated by the development team.

**How to apply:** Prioritise organiser onboarding and posting readiness. Treat
uploaded opportunity lists as reference unless the user explicitly requests an
import; do not invent missing dates, capacities, approvals or organiser accounts.
