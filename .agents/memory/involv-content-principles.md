---
name: Involv content principles
description: Product language decisions for opportunity value and student-facing messaging.
---

Involv should present every meaningful service-learning opportunity as valuable; do not classify listings with a separate CV-quality or CV-relevance label.

**Why:** The user considers all meaningful service-learning experience relevant, and the label can imply that some opportunities matter less.

**How to apply:** Prefer concrete skills, impact, responsibilities, and experience details when explaining an opportunity's value.
