---
name: Reflection access boundary
description: Student-owned reflections with narrowly scoped owner/admin read access.
---

Student reflections are student-owned notes, not graded or teacher-reviewed
submissions. The current task explicitly permits the approved activity owner
and verified school admins to read relevant reflections; classmates and
unrelated educators must not read them. Only authors may edit or delete.

**Why:** The explicit reflection task requires limited educator read access,
superseding the earlier author-only interpretation of “private.”

**How to apply:** Explain this visibility before students save. Restrict educator
reads by current verified identity, approved role and activity ownership, not
generic educator status. Keep any feedback/grading system separate. Do not claim
end-to-end encryption or protection from the privileged database operator.

Personal reflection history must remain accessible to its author independently
of current activity membership, without reopening the former group's workspace.
Account-closure retention remains a separate school-approved decision.

**Why:** Private notes belong to the student's personal history; losing group
membership should not trap those notes or force restored access to a roster/chat.

**How to apply:** Keep personal-history access narrowly author-scoped. Do not
infer an account-deletion retention policy from reflection or activity behavior.
