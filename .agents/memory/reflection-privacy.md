---
name: Private reflection product boundary
description: Personal reflections are not teacher-reviewed submissions or a shared activity resource.
---

Treat student reflections as personal notes, not teacher-reviewed submissions.
Activity ownership and the school-admin application role must not implicitly
grant access to another student's reflection text.

**Why:** The user requested private student reflections. The older UI prototype
let organisers see everyone's reflections, which conflicts with that scope.

**How to apply:** Keep any future teacher-sharing/review feature separate and
explicitly authorised; do not silently widen private-note policies to support
it. Describe privacy as author-only access through Involv, not as end-to-end
encryption or protection from the privileged database operator.

Personal reflection history must remain accessible to its author independently
of current activity membership, without reopening the former group's workspace.
Account-closure retention remains a separate school-approved decision.

**Why:** Private notes belong to the student's personal history; losing group
membership should not trap those notes or force restored access to a roster/chat.

**How to apply:** Keep personal-history access narrowly author-scoped. Do not
infer an account-deletion retention policy from reflection or activity behavior.
